Privacy Policy

Placeholder privacy policy for this demo site. Final wording is confirmed with the business before launch.

1. What we collect

When you ring or email about a job, we collect the details needed to do the work: your name, contact number, location and information about the vehicle or machine.

2. How we use it

Contact details are used to quote, book and carry out tyre work, and to follow up on jobs. They are not sold or passed to third parties for marketing.

3. Storage

Job records are kept only as long as needed for warranty, accounting and tax purposes under New Zealand law.

4. This website

This site has no contact forms and sets no tracking cookies of its own. The embedded Google Map is served by Google and is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask what personal information we hold about you and request a correction. Ring 027 461 2572 to ask.
