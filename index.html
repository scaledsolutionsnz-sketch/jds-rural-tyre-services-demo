South Waikato, our patch

A tyre truck built for farm country

JDs Rural Tyre Services runs out of Tokoroa on State Highway 1, right in the middle of dairy, forestry and cartage country. The whole outfit is set up mobile, so the tyre shop comes up your driveway instead of your machine limping into town.

One call to Jamie gets a straight answer on what it needs, what it costs, and when he can be there.
